Strong Password Generator

A fresh 14-character password, generated locally with your browser's cryptographic random number generator. Nothing leaves this page.

Password requirements

  • Exactly 14 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter
  • At least one lowercase letter
  • At least one number
  • At least one special character
  • No ambiguous characters (O 0 I l 1 | `)
  • No spaces
  • No repeated (aaa) or sequential (abc, 123) patterns

Passwords are generated with crypto.getRandomValues() and never stored or transmitted.
